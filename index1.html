🧠 What is Cyber Security?

Cyber security is the practice of protecting computers, networks, applications, devices and data from unauthorized access, attacks, damage and disruption. It combines technology, processes and human awareness to keep digital systems safe.

CONFIDENTIALITYOnly authorized people can access information.
INTEGRITYInformation must remain accurate and trustworthy.
